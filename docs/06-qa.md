## 6. Coverage statement

Business logic is now covered by direct tests: pricing, order numbering/transactions, tracking, customer
aggregation, invoices, payments, RBAC, SEO scoring, phone normalization, rate limits. UI components and
server-function wiring are exercised through SSR smoke and three live RPC calls, not a coverage tool.

**Quality gate 3 is not formally certified** (no `coverage-v8`, no browser E2E), but the order lifecycle is
materially better covered than the previous delivery.
