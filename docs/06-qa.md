## 5. Coverage statement

Coverage is measured by test inventory for pure business logic, not by a coverage tool:

- `src/lib/pricing.ts` — 5/5 branches covered (sale, percent, fixed, expired, shipping).
- `src/lib/admin-rules.ts` — all predicates, including self/super-admin protection.
- `src/lib/whatsapp.ts` — message content, optional fields, encoding, tracking.
- `checkout.tsx` / `track-order.tsx` — redirect handlers are not unit-covered (no DOM E2E yet).

**Quality gate 3 (80 % business logic) is NOT certified** until a coverage tool and a browser E2E
run are added.
